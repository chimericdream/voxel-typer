Themes (`band=all`): blocks 37, animals 53, colors 23, food 38, tools 32, nature 36.

How the bands relate: a Miner pool repeats its biome's Sprout words and adds more. A Builder pool keeps the Miner words of 4+ letters and adds longer ones (grade 5–6+: `hypothesis`, `punctuation`, `precipitation`). The validator rejects a word that appears in two pools of the same band, so in each band the Birch, Shore and Frosty pools share no word. Sprout lists also skip every word already in `sight-dolch.txt`.

## Decisions

- **Meadow shortfall.** The keys `a s d f g h j k l ;` spell only about 45 safe, readable words. `home-row.txt` already has 43 of them (`band=all`), and only 8 Dolch words can be typed with them. Sprout 60, Miner 120 and Builder 200 can't be met with real words. I didn't pad the pools with nonsense or near-duplicates. Meadow gets more items from phrases instead: `meadow-phrases` (29, M2-12) plus the new `meadow-gh-phrases` (35).
- **Meadow g/h phrases are in their own file.** M2-12 merged while this card was in flight. `meadow-phrases.txt` is tagged `keys=asdfjkl;`, and its lesson 06 tests cite it, so it can't take `g` or `h`. `meadow-gh-phrases.txt` doesn't clash with M2-12's file names, and every item has a `g` or an `h`, so it can't duplicate one of theirs. M2-12's lessons name their pools (`home-row`, `meadow-phrases`), so the new `biome=meadow` pool doesn't change them. I left `fad`, `fads` and `falls` in `home-row.txt`. Changing lesson 05 belongs to M3-03.
- **Reading tags.** The card says Sprout `dolch`/`cvc`/`grade1`, but the validator has no `grade1`, and Sprout pools may use `dolch`, `cvc` or `grade2`. Sprout lists are `grade2`, Miner `grade4` and Builder `grade6+`. Theme lists are `cvc` (blocks, animals) or `grade2`.
- **Theme lists are `band=all`** with no `biome` tag. A word can appear both in a theme list and in a biome list, because they are different bands. Theme lists don't share words with each other or with `home-row.txt`.
- **Shore lists may hold a few words without Shore keys** (`shell`, `tide`, `sail`, `seagull`) for flavor. Frosty's key set is the same as Shore's (a–z), so the two are separated by vocabulary and theme only.
- **Left out on purpose, beyond the blocklist** (full list in `SOURCES.md`):
  - Words with adult slang senses: `buns`, `tart`, `chick`, `fruity`, `frigid`, `snuggle`.
  - Product or IP names: `jeep`, `tweet`, `superhero`, `explorer` (a Microsoft product name), `potter`, and `asteroids` (the plural is a game title).
  - Fear or hurt words, and words for fire.
  - The whole `expl…` family, explosion words included, as the brief asked.
  - Sprout words too hard for a 6-year-old to read: `reed`, `quail`, `scuba`, `lynx` and others.
- **How the lists were made.** I wrote candidate words by hand. A scratch script (not committed) checked each one for key set, band length, Dolch and home-row overlap and same-band duplicates, then sorted them and wrote the files. The validator found no blocklist hits.

## Notes for downstream tasks

- **M3-03 (lessons):** a biome's default pools come from its `biome=<id>` tag. Birch lessons pick from `birch-*` (filtered to the player's band, plus `band=all`). Phrase lessons should name `<biome>-phrases-<band>` or rely on `kind=phrases`. Meadow lessons after `g h` can add `meadow-gh-phrases`.
- **Bundle size:** these lists add about 29 KB of raw text, which shrinks a lot with gzip. They ship once something imports `pools.ts`. Today nothing in the app bundle does, so `npm run size` is unchanged.
- The lists are sorted alphabetically. The generator samples them, so the order doesn't matter, but appends should keep the sort to keep diffs readable.
