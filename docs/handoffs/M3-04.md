## What changed

- `src/content/worldgen/islands.ts` (pure): `generateIsland(biome, seed)` builds one 16×12×16 voxel model per biome: a clump of ground that tapers underneath, with landmarks on top (a red-roofed house on Meadow Home, birches, a lagoon with a boat, snowy peaks, a mine mouth, tall mushrooms, glowing crystals, a brick works, a keep on a cloud). Also `ISLAND_KINDS` (37 map-only voxel kinds built from the shared texture recipes), `ISLAND_LAYOUT` (where each island floats) and `ISLAND_LINKS` (which island the stepping stones come from).
- `src/render/map/tiles.ts`: one small atlas for all nine islands, each painted from its own biome palette (`paletteFor`). A locked island gets washed-out copies of its tiles.
- `src/render/map/geometry.ts`: island meshes through the shared `meshChunk` (AO) and `splitByBucket`; a merged-boxes geometry for the cloud cover and the stepping stones.
- `src/render/map/view.ts`: `createWorldMapView(stage, opts)`. It adds the islands to the app's shared scene, moves the camera between the overview and a close view of one island, lifts the picked island, reports where each label goes on screen, and removes and frees everything in `dispose()`.
- `src/ui/screens/WorldMap.tsx` + `WorldMap.module.css`: the screen. One DOM button per island, placed under its island; the heading, a hint and "Change player". Without a 3D scene the same buttons lay out flat.
- `src/ui/screens/BiomePath.tsx`: an island's lesson path: numbered stones with 0–3 stars, the next one tagged "Next" and pulsing, lessons not open yet hatched.
- `src/ui/screens/map-logic.ts`: island and lesson data from the profile (`islandInfos`, `lessonNodes`, `recommendedLesson`, `practiceCamp`, the screen-reader labels).
- `src/i18n/en/map.ts`: every string (new namespace, one import and one entry in `index.ts`).

## Decisions

- **Islands are models with their own voxel kinds, not registry blocks.** The registry has only Meadow's blocks today, so islands built from it could not show snow, mushrooms or crystals. The kinds use the shared recipes and each biome's palette, so nothing is duplicated and all nine biomes look right now.
- **Locked look:** washed-out tiles (82 % toward gray, then half toward a pale mist color) baked into the atlas, no shader. Three white puffs per locked island, all in one mesh. A locked island never glows. Its button shows the name only, hatched; its accessible name says which island to finish first.
- **The path is a panel beside the island, not stones drawn on the island.** Stars and numbers on voxel textures were hard to read. The camera puts the island in the left third and the path takes the right; in a narrow window the island is centered at the top and the path goes below.
- **Arrow keys follow curriculum order** (right/down next, left/up previous) over open islands, then "Change player", wrapping. The islands are laid out along a snaking path, so order matches the picture.
- **Escape:** out of a path first; from the whole map to "Who is playing?". The shell's own history would lead back to the lesson just played.
- **Coming back from a lesson** opens the same island's path once, with the cursor on the next lesson. If a new island opened meanwhile, the whole map shows instead.
- **Next lesson** (`recommendedLesson`): first open lesson not played, else the open lesson with the fewest stars below three, else none. A lesson is open when it is first in an unlocked biome or the one before it has been played.
- **Button names are hidden text, not `aria-label`.** axe's `label-content-name-mismatch` fails an `aria-label` that doesn't contain the visible text. The name of the recommended island ends with "Go here next!".
- **Motion:** a 0.9 s glide onto the overview, 0.65 s in and out of an island, a 0.16 s lift of the picked island. Nothing loops in 3D. Reduced motion snaps all of it and stops the DOM pulse.
- **The archipelago is the same for everyone** (`MAP_SEED`), so the baselines hold and the map is a place a child can learn.
- **Sound:** one `whoosh` going in and out. No sound on cursor moves.
