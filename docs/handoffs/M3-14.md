## Decisions

- **The night theme on menus.** Menus only ever get day or high contrast
  (`themeFor(highContrast, "day")`), so the night theme can't be reached from Settings. The sweep
  copies what `applyTheme` writes from the UI kit page (`/ui-kit.html`, one section per theme)
  onto the page's root, with no test switch in the app. High contrast through the real setting is
  also covered (`a11y.spec.ts`, `settings.spec.ts`, the persistence test). If menus should ever
  follow the scene's time of day, that's a product call, not this card's.
- **Placement in the flow is played, not skipped:** a 16 WPM typist with no slips. The test
  accepts Birch Forest or Sandy Shore as the start, since CI's speed varies and both open Birch.
- **Toast order is checked as "at least 950 ms after the last page message"**, 50 ms under
  `TOAST_AFTER_PAGE_MS` for clock rounding. Badge toasts also wait `BADGE_SETTLE_MS` (1.5 s) on a
  new screen. Even so, the CI run showed they could be read together before the fix above.
