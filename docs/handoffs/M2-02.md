## What changed

- `src/content/biomes/env.ts` (pure): the env data types (`SkyStops`, `TimeEnv`, `DiscEnv`, `AmbientParticle`, `BiomeEnv`), `BIOME_ENVS`, `biomeEnv(biome)` (biomes without data fall back to the Meadow), `TIMES_OF_DAY`, `DEFAULT_T01 = 0.3`, `celestialFor(tod, t01)` (where the sun and moon discs sit), and two small color helpers (`hexToRgb01`, `relativeLuminance`) for tests.
- `src/content/biomes/meadow.ts` (env part): `MEADOW_ENV`. For each time of day it has the sky stops, fog density, sun color and intensity, hemisphere colors and intensity, cloud color, and sun and moon disc colors and opacity. It also has `pollen` as the ambient particle.
- `src/render/sky.ts`: `createSkyEnvironment(opts) → (ctx) => SkyController`. Pass it as `SceneOptions.createEnvironment`. It adds:
  - a gradient sky dome: a `ShaderMaterial` with three stops (zenith, horizon, ground). The dome follows the camera and is drawn first, with no depth test or write.
  - a square sun and moon: flat quads with an 8×8 `NearestFilter` pixel texture, tinted by the env color. They are drawn in the transparent pass with a depth test, so terrain covers a low sun.
  - `FogExp2`, colored like the horizon stop.
  - the clouds.
  - the light values, written into `ctx.lights`.

  `set()` cross-fades everything over 2 s, and the system returns `true` only while it fades or the clouds drift. The controller also has `setDrift`, `drifting`, `fading`, `mix`, `clouds`, `dome`, `sunDisc` and `moonDisc`.

- `src/render/clouds.ts`: `createClouds({ seed, count, lowCount })`. One `InstancedMesh` of flat boxes (38 clouds of 1–3 slabs each, one draw call). The layout is seeded. The faces carry baked shade as vertex colors. The clouds drift along +x, wrap around a 320-block band, and write their matrices in place.
- `tests/unit/render/env.test.ts` (20 tests): env data validation, `celestialFor`, a deterministic cloud layout with wrapping, and the sky controller. The controller tests cover the snap before the first frame, the 2 s fade that settles the light rig, the fallback biome, `frozen`, drift and reduced motion, and dispose.
- Outside Owns, needed for the acceptance:
  - `src/dev/scene-preview.ts`: the preview page now uses the sky. New parameters `?t01=` and `?sky=flat` (the old flat background). New hook `vtScene.sky`.
  - `tests/e2e/sky.spec.ts` (new): 8 frozen screenshots (4 times of day × `overview`/`mine`), each checked for an idle loop and the draw-call budget. It also has three behavior tests: clouds drift only while live, reduced motion idles, and a cross-fade runs about 2 s and then idles.
  - `tests/e2e/__screenshots__/scene.spec.ts/scene.png` deleted, so the workflow makes it again with the sky.

## Decisions

- **Day lighting unchanged.** The Meadow day values are M2-01's (sun `#fff4e0` 2.6, hemisphere `#dcefff`/`#b8a888` 1.0, no tone mapping), and a unit test pins them. I tuned dawn, dusk and night:

  |       | sun light     | hemisphere (sky / ground / intensity) | sky (zenith / horizon / ground)   |
  | ----- | ------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
  | dawn  | `#ffc79c` 2.0 | `#f1d6c8` / `#8f7c6a` / 1.0           | `#7388c6` / `#f3bc93` / `#c4b4c4` |
  | day   | `#fff4e0` 2.6 | `#dcefff` / `#b8a888` / 1.0           | `#4f92d6` / `#b6dbef` / `#9cc6e4` |
  | dusk  | `#ffae7a` 1.8 | `#dcaaa8` / `#7d6662` / 1.0           | `#5c5ea2` / `#ef9c70` / `#a98a9c` |
  | night | `#a6b8ff` 1.0 | `#7487c4` / `#3e4466` / 1.15          | `#22306a` / `#46588f` / `#36447a` |

- **Night stays readable.** Every night sky stop has a WCAG relative luminance of at least 0.03, and the night zenith is a mid navy (`#22306a`), not black. Moonlight and the hemisphere light add up to 2.15, so blocks keep their shape and color. A unit test enforces both.
- **The light direction stays fixed.** `SUN_DIR` (M2-01) is the same at every hour; only the color and strength change. The drawn sun and moon follow `celestialFor`, which keeps them low (2–12°) and within ±30° of straight ahead, where the `mine` camera sees them. Moving the light with the time of day would change the voxel shading the review approved, and `lights.fitTo` uses the constant `SUN_DIR`. The drawn sun therefore doesn't line up with the shadows. That's deliberate.
- **What `t01` means.** I read it as progress through the given time of day (0 = start, 1 = end): the sun rises through dawn, crosses during the day and sinks at dusk. It defaults to 0.3 when left out and is pinned at 0.3 under `frozen`, as `?freeze` says. `src/render/types.ts` calls it "the position in the day/night cycle". That fits my reading, because the time of day is passed alongside it. See Questions.
- **Two cloud layers.** The `overview` camera looks down at about 15–50° below the horizon, so it never sees the sky above the horizon. High clouds (14 above the diorama's top, behind it on −z) show in the `mine` view. Low clouds (about y = −9, below the diorama's base) show in `overview` and make the diorama read as a floating island. One `InstancedMesh` holds both layers. The dome's ground stop is a lower-sky blue, not earth, for the same reason.
- **When `set()` cross-fades and when it snaps.** It snaps until the first frame is drawn. The scene calls `env.set(day)` in its constructor, and without this rule the app's first real `setEnvironment` would fade from that default. It also always snaps under `frozen`. Setting the same biome, time and `t01` again does nothing. Under reduced motion the colors still fade, because a fade isn't motion, but the sun and moon jump to their new place instead of gliding.
- **The idle rule.** Clouds drift only while `drift` is on (the default), the scene isn't `frozen`, and reduced motion is off. The system returns `true` only while drifting or fading, so a frozen or reduced-motion scene idles (e2e checks `loop.scheduled === false`). If reduced motion turns off later, drift resumes on the next requested frame.
- **No allocation per frame.** The mix state, the colors and the vectors are made once. The cross-fade lerps in place, and the clouds reuse one `Matrix4`. The light rig's `apply()` (which makes strings) runs only when a fade ends.
- **The clouds center on the diorama** through `ctx.lights.sun.target.position`, which `fitTo` puts at the diorama's middle. `EnvironmentContext` has no diorama bounds, and reading the target avoids changing the M2-01 seam.
- **The pollen particle is data only.** The card lists the ambient particle under "Biome env data", and nothing renders it yet (see Notes).
- **Original look.** The sky colors are my own picks and don't match another game's sky colors. The sun is a two-tone square with a lighter core, the moon a pale square with a few darker crater pixels, and the clouds are flat stacked slabs.

## Notes for downstream tasks

- **M2-10 (app shell):** pass `createEnvironment: createSkyEnvironment({ frozen: debug.freeze })` to `createSceneHandle`. Without it the scene keeps M2-01's flat background. The clouds drift by default, so a live scene draws every frame while the sky is on. If a screen should idle (menus, results), keep the controller from the factory and call `sky.setDrift(false)`. Under `?freeze` pass `frozen: true`, together with the fixed-step clock M2-01 already asks for.
- **Whoever drives day/night** (a mode, or a later cycle task) calls `setEnvironment({ biome, timeOfDay, t01 })`. Changing `t01` within a time of day moves the sun with a 2 s glide. Every call starts a 2 s fade, so call it when the value changes, not every frame.
- **M2-03 (meadow worldgen)** also owns part of `src/content/biomes/meadow.ts`. This PR creates the file with only `MEADOW_ENV`. Add the worldgen export next to it.
- **The ambient pollen particle isn't rendered.** `MEADOW_ENV.particle` holds `{ kind: 'pollen', color, density }`. A small pooled `InstancedMesh` like M2-05's particles could draw it over the diorama footprint (drifting, and frozen under `?freeze` or reduced motion). No card owns that yet.
- **Draw calls:** the sky adds up to 4: the dome, the clouds and the visible disc(s). A hidden disc (opacity < 0.01, or below the horizon) costs nothing.
- **Fog** is `FogExp2` at 0.0035–0.0045. Lambert chunk materials take it automatically, and on the 48³ test grid it barely touches the diorama. A biome with a hazier look (shore, marsh) can raise `fogDensity`.
