# M2-03 Meadow diorama

Status: done. The visual review is still to come. The screenshot baselines (`meadow-overview.png`, `meadow-mine.png`) come from the Update snapshots workflow.

## What changed

- `src/content/worldgen/noise.ts`: lattice value noise (`hash2`, `valueNoise2`), `fbm2` (2 octaves), `terrace`, and a seeded Bridson Poisson-disk sampler (`poissonDisk`). The sampler uses no trig, so its output can't drift between JS engines.
- `src/content/worldgen/diorama.ts`:
  - `generateDiorama(biome, seed, size = DIORAMA_SIZE) → VoxelGrid` (64×24×64).
  - `dioramaStage(biome, size)` gives the stage: the floor height, the target cell and the clear volume. It depends on the biome and size only, never the seed.
  - `recipeFor(biome)` and the `DioramaRecipe` type.
  - Block nums come from the registry by id (the M2-04 group files), never hard-coded.
- `src/content/worldgen/templates/*.ts`: `types.ts` (a `Template` is a voxel list that follows either its anchor or each column's ground), `trees.ts` (3 original oak shapes: `puff` round crown, `spire` tiered crown, `twin` forked trunk with two crowns), `flowers.ts` (loose single-kind patches with a stray second kind), `rocks.ts` (low cobble and mossy-cobble clusters), `fence.ts` (log posts every 3 cells with a plank rail and open gaps below).
- `src/content/biomes/meadow.ts`: added a separate "Worldgen part (task M2-03)" section below M2-02's `MEADOW_ENV`, exporting `MEADOW_WORLDGEN`, plus one `import type` line at the top. M2-02's section is unchanged.
- Tests: `tests/unit/worldgen/noise.test.ts` and `tests/unit/worldgen/diorama.test.ts`.
- **Outside Owns** (render and dev code, kept small and next to M2-01's code):
  - `src/render/water.ts` (new): the water material, a transparent Lambert material with a top-face vertex wobble (`patchWaterShader`), and its loop system.
  - `src/render/chunks.ts`: adds a fourth bucket, `water`, for `liquid`-tagged blocks. Water now has its own mesh, apart from glass, which fixes the sort problem M2-01 described. Water meshes cast no shadows. `splitByBucket` now loops over `BUCKETS` instead of a hard-coded 3.
  - `src/render/scene.ts`: makes the water material, maps `liquid` to `water` in `bucketOf`, adds the water system to the loop, and adds a new option `waterMotion?: boolean` (default true).
  - `src/dev/scene-preview.ts`: adds to the page without replacing anything:
    - `?world=meadow` loads the generated diorama with the real block registry, through the same sky (M2-02) and FX (M2-05) wiring. A stand-in `meadowstone` floats on the stage; `?target=0` hides it.
    - `?still=1` turns off the camera drift and leaves the water wobble on.
    - `waterMotion: !freeze`.
    - New hooks `stage` and `meshes()`.
    - FX demo bursts use the stand-in's block id.
  - `tests/unit/render/chunks.test.ts`: water bucket cases. `tests/unit/render/water.test.ts` (new).
  - `tests/e2e/diorama.spec.ts` (new): 4 tests on `scene.html?world=meadow`.

## Decisions

- **Water uses `glass` as its `Material`.** This is the registry's `water` block from M2-04, so there is no contract change. Rendering keys off the block's `liquid` tag, not its Material.
- **The water wobble never wakes the loop.** Its system copies the frame time into a uniform and returns `false`, so the pond moves only while something else is already drawing (a mode's `onFrame`, the clouds, a tween). Reduced motion or `waterMotion: false` sets the amplitude to 0. Top faces always sit 0.12 below the block top, so the pond reads as water inside its sand rim.
- **Layout follows the camera.** The camera sits on +z looking toward −z, so the stage goes 14 cells in from the front edge, centered in x. Behind it the terrain rises up to 4 blocks toward the back (`backRise`), and most trees stand there. The pond goes off to one side in the back half, level with the stage. A path leads from just behind the target toward the pond. A fence runs beside the stage, 8 cells off-center, with a gap where the path crosses it.
- **No drops near the stage.** The heights are clamped to slope 1: lift any dip next to a fixed cell, then take the largest 1-Lipschitz surface under the result, using two-sweep Manhattan distance transforms. The stage floor and the pond rim stay exact.
- **Stage API.** The card's `→ VoxelGrid` return type is kept. The stage lives in a separate, seed-independent `dioramaStage()`, so M2-13 can place its target without regenerating.
- **Unknown biomes** fall back to the Meadow recipe until M3 adds its own.
- **No new dev page.** The orchestrator asked me to add to the existing page rather than replace it, so I didn't make a `diorama.html`: the meadow is `scene.html?world=meadow`, which reuses the sky and FX wiring.
