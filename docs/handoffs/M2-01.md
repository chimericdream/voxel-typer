# M2-01 Render core

Status: done. Waiting on the visual review. The screenshot baseline (`scene.png`) comes from the Update snapshots workflow on this PR.

## What changed

- `src/render/scene.ts`: `createSceneHandle(canvas, opts) → SceneCore`. `SceneCore` is the contract's `SceneHandle` plus `dispose()`, `renderStats()`, `resize()`, `setVoxel()`, `markDirty()`, and getters for `chunks()`, `grid()` and `atlas()`. It uses a WebGL2 renderer with `antialias: false`, sRGB output, `NoToneMapping` and `BasicShadowMap`. Three shared materials: opaque Lambert, transparent Lambert (`depthWrite: false`) and an unlit "glow" material for `light` blocks. The atlas is built from `opts.blocks` with `paletteFor(biome)` and rebuilt when the biome changes. Two seams keep later tasks out of this file: `opts.createFx` (M2-05) and `opts.createEnvironment` (M2-02).
- `src/render/loop.ts`: `createLoop(deps)`. It runs rAF only while there is an `onFrame` subscriber, a busy system (camera tween, drift or shake) or an active hit-stop. Otherwise each `requestRender()` draws one frame. It reads the clock once per frame, clamps `dt` to 50 ms, and the first frame after idle gets `dt = 0`. During `hitStop(ms)`, subscribers get `dt = 0` while systems keep real time. Subscriber errors go to `onError` and don't stop the loop.
- `src/render/chunks.ts`: `createChunkStore(...)` meshes 16³ chunks with `meshChunk`, one `BufferGeometry` per chunk and bucket. `splitByBucket` splits the mesher's single mesh into opaque, transparent and glow parts by the block behind each quad. `markDirty(x,y,z)` queues the cell's chunk plus every neighbor that reads it, diagonals included, because AO samples them. `flush()` rebuilds queued chunks and frees the old geometries.
- `src/render/lights.ts`: the sun (a `DirectionalLight` whose tight shadow frustum `fitTo(box)` fits around the diorama, shadow map only on `high`, 1024²), a `HemisphereLight`, and lamp point lights nearest the camera focus, capped by quality. `DEFAULT_LIGHTING` holds the per-time-of-day defaults.
- `src/render/camera.ts`: an orbit-pose rig with presets `mine` and `overview` (`lane`, `orbit` and `rail` use `overview` until their modes exist) and ease-in-out cubic tweens. It also has `orbit()`, ±0.5° yaw / ±0.25° pitch parallax drift, and trauma-model `shake()`. Extra methods: `setBounds`, `setTarget`, `setDrift`, `pose()`.
- `src/render/quality.ts`: `QUALITY_PRESETS` (pixel ratio cap, shadows, shadow map size, point lights, post) and `pixelRatioFor(q, dpr)`.
- `src/render/index.ts`: the barrel exports the new modules.
- Dev page (outside Owns, as the acceptance needs): `scene.html` + `src/dev/scene-preview.ts`. A seeded 48×24×48 test grid (3×2×3 chunks) with terraced terrain, a pond (water), a stepped brick pyramid, a plank arch, a stone hut with a glass window and a lamp inside, trees, random scatter, and an ore block on a pedestal as the Mine target. Parameters: `?seed`, `?freeze=1`, `?quality`, `?preset=overview|mine`, `?tod`, `?reducedMotion=1`. It exposes `window.vtScene` for e2e.
- `tests/e2e/scene.spec.ts`: the frozen screenshot (`scene.png`) plus checks for no console errors, a 1280×720 canvas, an idle loop, draw calls in budget and a non-trivial frame. Also the dispose check, and a smoke test of `mine` + `high`.
- `tests/unit/dom/render-loop.test.ts`: 12 loop tests with a fake rAF.
- `tests/unit/render/chunks.test.ts` and `tests/unit/render/camera.test.ts` (outside Owns; Owns lists no test file for chunks or camera, and the acceptance asks for a chunks test): 14 chunk-store tests and 10 camera/quality tests.
- `vite.config.ts` (outside Owns, see Decisions): adds `scene.html` as a build input in test mode only.

## Decisions

- **No tone mapping (`NoToneMapping`)**, sRGB output. Palette colors stay flat and saturated, as the card asks. The light intensities are tuned so a top face in daylight lands near the palette color: `sun 1.6`, `hemisphere 1.5`, sky `#dcefff`, ground `#a89878`. My first values (2.2/1.6) clipped untextured top faces to white and washed out the AO.
- **Hard shadows (`BasicShadowMap`)** suit the pixel look. Shadows are on `high` only, per the card, although PLAN §5.1 says "quality ≥ medium". The card wins.
- **Light blocks always glow.** Faces of `light`-tagged blocks go in a third bucket with an unlit `MeshBasicMaterial` (AO kept) at every quality. On `low` that is the card's "emissive tint instead". On `medium` and `high`, point lights (4 and 8) also go to the lamps nearest the camera focus. Point light intensity is `BlockDef.light.intensity × 3`, with range 8 and decay 2.
- **Bucket split by quad owner.** `meshChunk` returns one mesh, and M1-13 suggested a per-pass lookup wrapper. Instead, I mesh once and assign each quad to the block half a unit behind its center along the normal. That keeps one meshing pass per chunk and needs no contract change.
- **Dirty neighborhood includes diagonals.** A cell on a chunk edge or corner also marks the diagonal neighbor chunks, because the AO of their faces samples that cell. A corner edit therefore rebuilds 8 chunks.
- **Reduced motion:** preset changes snap instead of tweening, drift is off, `shake` and `hitStop` do nothing. The card only names drift. I also snap tweens because a long camera glide is the kind of motion reduced-motion users avoid. `reducedMotion` is a getter, so a settings change applies at once.
- **Drift is on by default** (`ambientMotion`), so a scene with drift renders continuously. The dev page turns it off under `?freeze`. The app (M2-10) should turn it off where render-on-demand matters (static menus over a scene) with `camera.setDrift(false)`.
- **Hit-stop counts real (unclamped) frame time**, and the first frame after idle (dt 0) doesn't use any of it up.
- **The default FX** does only `hitStop`. `burst` and `floatText` are no-ops until M2-05 passes `createFx`. `shake` lives on the camera rig (contract `CameraRig.shake`) and uses the trauma model from the M2-05 card: `trauma += intensity`, offset = trauma² × noise, decay 1.5/s, capped by `durationMs`. Its noise comes from deterministic sines, with no `Math.random`.
- **`vite.config.ts` changed (outside Owns, the smallest change).** `scene.html` is a build input only when `mode === "test"`, next to `atlas.html`. `dist/` has no `scene.html` (checked).
- **The test grid uses its own preview blocks** (14 blocks with M1-14 recipes) until M2-04's registry lands. `atlas-preview.ts`'s `SAMPLE_BLOCKS` isn't exported, and that file is M1-14's.
- **The dev hook is `window.vtScene`**, not `__scene`, because Oxlint's `no-underscore-dangle` flags `__scene`.

## Notes for downstream tasks

- **M2-10 (app shell):** create one handle per canvas with `createSceneHandle(canvas, { blocks, seed, quality, reducedMotion, clock, ambientMotion })`. Pass `clock: fixedStepClock(0, 16)` and `ambientMotion: false` under `?freeze`. Wire `__vt` draw calls to `handle.renderStats().calls`. Note that `renderer.info.render` resets on each render, so read it after a frame. Call `handle.dispose()` on teardown. `reducedMotion` must resolve `Settings.reducedMotion` (`'system'` → `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')`).
- **M2-02 (sky):** pass `createEnvironment(ctx) → { set(env), system?, dispose() }`. `ctx.lights.apply(state)` sets sun and hemisphere, and `ctx.scene` takes your sky dome and fog. Return `true` from `system` while lerping or while clouds drift. The atlas palette swap on a biome change is already handled in the scene.
- **M2-05 (FX):** pass `createFx(ctx)`. `ctx` has `scene`, `camera`, `renderer`, `canvas`, `loop` (`hitStop`, `addSystem`, `wake`), `cameraRig` (`shake`), `reducedMotion()` and `atlas()`. The contract's `fx.hitStop` should call `ctx.loop.hitStop(ms)` unless reduced motion is on. Systems return `true` while particles are alive.
- **M2-03 / M4 Builder:** `setVoxel(x,y,z,num)` edits the current diorama and rebuilds the touched chunks next frame. After writing to `grid()` directly, call `markDirty`. The water wobble shader can replace `materials.transparent`, or water can get its own bucket. That's a small change in `scene.ts`, so ask.
- Draw calls on the 48×24×48 test grid: 15 at medium (13 in the mine view) and 26 at high (shadow pass). Empty chunks add no mesh.
- `camera.setTarget(cellCenter(x,y,z))` then `preset('mine')` frames a Mine target block. The camera sits on +z and looks toward −z, so put the scenic backdrop at lower z.
