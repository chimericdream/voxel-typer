## Decisions

- **One color per finger type, the same on both hands.** Five colors (pinky pink, ring cyan, middle lime, pointer amber, thumb pale blue) are easier to keep apart for colorblind kids than eight. The hands are told apart by position, by the mirrored pointer stripes in the middle, by the hand overlay, and by the caption ("left pointer finger"). Colors come from `BASE_PALETTE` ramps, like the rest of the theme.
- **Patterns are one shared semi-opaque shade (`zonePattern`) over each zone color**, drawn as CSS gradients in 3-px steps (the bevel unit), so they look like pixel art. One token means one contrast check per zone.
- **Glyphs use `--font-body` (Atkinson)**, per M2-08. The board overrides `KeyCap`'s font, size and border image with a `.slot > kbd` selector instead of forking the component. It drops the border image's `fill`, so the zone shows in the key face and the kit's bevel frame stays.
- **Letter keys print capitals**, like the physical keyboard the child looks down at. Number and symbol keys print both chars, Shift char on top.
- **Finger labels**: shown on the lit key(s) always, on hover, and on every key with `labels`. Each sits on a plain patch of the zone color, so the small text is never over the pattern. "pointer" is the Sprout word for the index finger.
- **Next key (changed after the a11y review, B1):** a two-tone ring painted over the key's own bevel frame. The outer band is `selected` and the inner band is `keycapText`, the dark glyph color. Then comes a 3-px lift, and a gentle bob when motion is on.
  - The inner band meets every zone face at 4.5:1 or more (the glyph pairs). The outer band meets the plate at 3:1 or more, and the two bands differ by at least 3:1. So the ring never depends on one color: the first ring (one `selected` band) was 1.00:1 on the pointer zone in day and on the ring zone in high contrast.
  - The ring sits inside the key, not over its neighbours. That fixes N1: it no longer covers the label of the key above.
  - In grayscale the lit key reads as a dark frame inside a light one.
  - The lit finger in the hand overlay gets the same two tones: a dark edge inside the `selected` glow.
- **Wrong-key wobble.** The key the child pressed wobbles. If that char isn't on the layout, the key they needed wobbles instead. It is never red. The cue is a dashed outline in `plateText` for 420 ms, plus a small rotation wobble that is multiplied by `--motion-ok`, so under reduced motion only the outline shows. The wobble animates `rotate` and the lift animates `translate`, which are separate properties. The ring is a pseudo-element, not the outline. So a wobble on the next key keeps its ring and lift (N4). The gate (`feedWobble`):
  - At most one wobble every 600 ms, which is under 2 a second (PLAN §4.6 allows up to 3 flashes a second).
  - The same key wobbles again only after 400 ms of quiet. A held key that auto-repeats wobbles once for the whole hold. A unit test holds a key for 5 s with 33 ms repeats and gets exactly 1 wobble.
  - A re-render with the same `wrong.seq` feeds nothing.
- **Key unit `--u` is `min(40px × --text-scale, --keyboard-max-unit, container width / 15)`**, in px, not rem. `[data-theme]` sets the root font size to `1.25rem × --text-scale`, so on `<html>`, `rem` already includes the text scale. Rem-based sizes would scale twice, and the first screenshots did. The caption and labels size from `--u` too.
- **en-GB's tall ISO Enter** is drawn as two pieces (rows 1 and 2), with its name on the top piece only.
- **The hands are original art**, drawn in code from rectangles on a 24 × 22 pixel grid. They are neutral "gloves" (the keycap face color), not skin, so they fit every avatar. No asset files.
- **The board is one `role="img"`** with an `aria-label` like "Next key: F. Use your left pointer finger. Hold shift with your right pinky." It is not live: announcing every key during typing would be noise. The caption under the hands is ordinary text. There's one `oxlint-disable-next-line jsx-a11y/prefer-tag-over-role` with a reason, like the kit's.

## Notes for downstream tasks

- **M2-14 (HUD) mounts it:**
  ```tsx
  <OnScreenKeyboard
    layout={layoutById(settings.layout) ?? EN_US}
    nextKey={hud.nextKey}
    show={settings.showKeyboard}
    hands={settings.showHands}
    wrong={wrongKey}
  />
  ```
  `wrong` is `{ got: string; seq: number }`. Pass a new `seq` for each wrong press: the engine's `wrong` event's `got`, with a counter. `HudState` has no field for this. A mode can put it in `HudState.custom` (for example `custom.wrongKey`), or the HUD can take it from the game events bus, whichever M2-14 prefers. The keyboard gates repeats itself, so passing every event is safe.
- **For M2-14's brief (review N2), height at text scale 2:** at Sprout scale (1.5) with hands, the keyboard with its plate is about 520 px tall at 1280×720. At scale 2 the review measured the page at 979 px tall at 1280×720 and 855 px at 1024×720. Width never overflows. Set `--keyboard-max-unit` (a px length) on an ancestor to cap the key size for the HUD layout. Suggested M2-14 acceptance item: "at text scale 2, the HUD with keyboard and hands fits 1280×720 and 1024×720 without vertical scroll".
- **For M3-07 (review N3), the finger-label toggle:** `labels` (finger labels on every key) is a prop, and nothing drives it yet. The labels show on the lit keys always, and on hover, but a keyboard-only child never hovers. M3-07 (Settings) should wire a setting to it, which needs a settings field, orchestrator-owned. M2-14 can also turn it on for Sprout.
- **M2-10 / M2-07, a gotcha, confirmed after the rebase:** `tokens.css` styles `[data-theme]` with `font-size: calc(var(--fs-md) * var(--text-scale))`, and M2-10's `src/main.ts` calls `applyTheme(document.documentElement, …)`. So `1rem` becomes `20px × text-scale`. Every kit size written as `Nrem × var(--text-scale)` (KeyCap, the `--fs-*` scale) then scales twice: at scale 2, `--fs-sm` is 80 px. Either theme a wrapper element instead of `<html>`, or drop the `font-size` from `[data-theme]`. The keyboard avoids `rem`, so it's safe either way.
- **M2-11 lesson intro** can reuse `targetFor` and `zoneOf` from `src/ui/keyboard/model.ts` for its "finger hints", and the `keyboard.finger.*` strings.
- **Adding a layout:** nothing to do here. The board fills modifier gaps by row, and the table test runs over `LAYOUTS` automatically.
