# M4-05 Wave director (pure)

Status: done, with one acceptance reading left open: 50 WPM is about 1 point over the band's top
(see Questions 1)

Night Defense's waves now come from a pure director. It plans each wave's mobs, lanes, spawn times,
speeds and words, then moves one difficulty scalar after the wave. The aim is to keep the child's
accuracy near 90 % with few mobs reaching the base. It reads no clock and no global randomness,
and nothing it returns can take anything from the child.

## Decisions

- **`d` is a time scale against the learner's own speed**, not an absolute pace. The reference is
  the baseline net WPM, clamped to 2–120. With no baseline it is a slow default of 5 / 10 / 15 WPM
  for Sprout / Miner / Builder. A night starts at `d = 0.8`, or 0.7 for a new profile. So
  `d ∈ [0.3, 2.0]` covers any typist the same way, and a baseline of 8 or 47 WPM both start near
  their edge.
- **Above `d = 1`, speed rises faster than the spawn rate.**
  - Spawn gap: `base / d` below 1, `base / d^0.3` above.
  - Travel time: `3 × base / d^1.5`, kept inside 4–40 s.

  More words a minute than a child can type swamps them, because each slip costs time. Shorter
  walking times raise the pressure without that. The plant sweep in `docs/sim.md` shows the cliff
  this avoids.

- **Search, then fine, plus relief** (module comment of `director.ts`):
  - Search: `k = 2.5`, steps within ±15 % until the first wave at or under 90 %.
  - Fine: `k = 1`, steps within −8 % / +5 %.
  - Relief: a wave under 75 %, or with more than 30 % of its mobs at the base, makes the next one
    15 % easier at once.
  - Accuracy is a words-weighted average (memory 0.5). The reached-base rule uses only the last
    wave.
- **Waves are sized by time, not mob count.** Spawns last about 20 / 25 / 30 s by band, plus 3 s a
  wave up to the fourth, with 3–18 mobs (calm at most 10). Fast learners get more mobs, so each
  wave has enough keys to measure, and no wave drags on for a slow learner.
- **Calm mode:**
  - a pace of 0.75×, a ceiling of `d = 1.2` and steps up of at most +8 %;
  - one wave of at most 10 mobs, with fast mobs half as likely;
  - `WaveSpec.calm` tells M4-04 to make mobs wait at the waiting line.

  The director has no timer to show. `spawnAtMs` and `speed` only drive the scene.

- **Words:**
  - one generator call per flavor per wave (`kind: "words"`, the band's lengths: Sprout 2–4,
    Miner 3–6, Builder 4–8);
  - 3 candidates per mob, so neighbours can start with different letters, which matters for
    `lockOn`;
  - no repeats within a wave while candidates last;
  - a flavor whose pools have fewer than 8 usable words uses the lesson's pools instead (learning
    first).

  Every word passes `typeable(word, allowed)`, has no spaces, and gets the learner's `keyWeights`
  when the caller passes them.

- **No trailing space in Defense, the same rule as Mine (decisions 81, 90).** Contracts §3.2 makes
  `trailingSpace` sequential-only. Checked against the engine: under `lockOn` a word completes on
  its last character even with `trailingSpace: true`. A space with nothing locked is a lockOn miss
  (`wrong`, `targetId: null`) that counts against accuracy and breaks the streak. So the words have
  no space, and M4-04 must drop a habit space the way Mine does (see below).
- **`WaveSpec` is an object, not a bare list** (`{ index, lanes, calm, difficulty, paceWpm, mobs }`).
  The mode needs the lane count and the calm flag with the mobs. It isn't in `contracts.md`, so
  it isn't a frozen contract. It lives in `director.ts`, which this card owns.
- **`DirectorOptions` goes beyond the card's `{ band, baselineNetWpm, calm, rng }`**, adding
  `mobs`, `pools`, `lessonPools`, `allowed`, `weights?` and `startDifficulty?`. The words need
  them, and nothing else could supply them. `Director.difficulty` is an extra read-only getter.
- **`speed` is in lane lengths per second.** M4-04 multiplies by its own lane length.

## Notes for downstream tasks

**M4-03 (mobs) must provide**, for each mob, a `DefenseMob`: `{ id, speedClass: 'slow' | 'medium'
| 'fast', flavor }`. `flavor` is one pool tag exactly as the pools carry it (`theme=snow`,
`theme=animals`, `biome=frosty`, …). The tags that exist today are `theme=animals | blocks |
colors | food | nature | tools` (band `all`) and the biome themes (`theme=snow`, `theme=forest`,
`theme=shore`, `theme=mines`, `theme=marsh`, `theme=caverns`, `theme=sky`, …). A flavor needs at
least 8 one-word items for the band, typeable with the lesson's keys, or the mob falls back to the
lesson's words. That is fine, but check it per biome. Export the roster in a form M4-04 can filter
by home biome. The sim uses stand-ins with guessed flavors (`SIM_MOBS` in `tools/sim-defense.ts`);
swap them for the real roster when it lands.

**M4-04 (Night Defense) must:**

1. Build the director per night:
   - `band`, `calm: settings.calmMode`;
   - `baselineNetWpm`: the same baseline as stars, contracts §6;
   - `rng: ctx.rng.fork('director')`;
   - `mobs`: the night's roster;
   - `pools`: every pool for the band;
   - `lessonPools`: `poolsFor(lesson, band, pools)` from `src/modes/mine/logic.ts`;
   - `allowed`: `allowedKeysFor(...)`;
   - `weights`: `keyWeights(...)`.
2. Play `wave.mobs` in `spawnAtMs` order. Each mob is a `lockOn` target
   `{ id: mob.id, text: mob.word, trailingSpace: false, priority }`, added with `addTargets` at
   spawn and removed with `removeTarget` when it bonks the shield. Use `wave.lanes` lanes.
   `1 / speed` is the seconds from horizon to base.
3. **Drop habit spaces.** A space with nothing locked, or before the first char of the locked word,
   must not reach the engine. Otherwise it is a lockOn miss: a wrong keystroke and a broken
   streak. This is Mine's `isStraySpace` rule (decision 90), and the same helper works.
4. After each wave, call `observe` with:
   - `accuracy`: correct ÷ keystrokes for that wave's keys, lockOn misses included;
   - `reachedBase`: mobs that bonked the shield;
   - `wordsDone`: mobs typed;
   - `activeMs`: the wave's unpaused time.

   A night is one engine session, so count these from the events between waves. Report
   `reachedBase` even while the shield is at zero (mobs bouncing off), because those mobs still got
   through.

5. **Calm mode** (`wave.calm`): mobs stop at the waiting line and never bonk, so `reachedBase` is 0. Show no clock or countdown anywhere.
6. Optional: keep `director.difficulty` in memory and pass it as `startDifficulty` for a second
   night in the same sitting. It isn't persisted: there's no save field, and none is asked for.

**M4-10:** the director has no path to inventory or progress. It returns plain data.
`controllerStep` never throws, and a wave in which nothing was typed (an idle child) makes
the next one easier, down to the 0.3 floor. There's a property test for that.

**M5 bosses (`minions` phase):** "director-scaled like Defense" can reuse `createDirector` or just
`controllerStep` and `mobTiming`.

## Contract change request / Questions

- No contract change. `WaveSpec` and the director types aren't in `contracts.md`.
- **Question 1 (reviewer, orchestrator): which reading of "converge … median over 50 seeds" holds
  for 50 WPM?**
  - Each learner's mean accuracy from wave 4 on: it is in band (89.5–90.8 %).
  - The median of each wave on its own: it peaks at 95.1–96.2 %.

  The cause is the plant, not the step size. A fast typist's accuracy at fixed `d` is flat at
  97–98 % and then falls about 11 points per 0.1 of `d`, so their waves are either fine or
  swamped. The test pins the learner mean in band and per-wave medians at or under 96.5 %. If the
  strict reading is required, the honest next lever is word difficulty for fast learners (longer
  or harder words as `d` rises). M1-07's typist can't measure that, because its slip rate doesn't
  depend on the word. Changing the pressure model until the table passes would only tune the sim,
  not the game.

- **Question 2 (feel review at M4-04):** the constants (reaction 1400/900/650 ms, travel 3×, wave
  length 20/25/30 s, lane counts) are first guesses chosen for the controller, not for feel. Tune
  them in the M4-04 feel review, then rerun `npm run tool -- sim-defense` and update
  `docs/sim.md`.
