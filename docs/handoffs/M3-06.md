# M3-06 Remedial lessons, baselines and band defaults

Status: done

## What changed

- `src/engine/remedial.ts` (new, pure; covered by the `src/engine/**` pure-zone override): the
  remedial rules.
  - `suggestRemedial(input)` returns a suggestion when an allowed key has weight ≥ 4 (contracts §4
    `keyWeights`, with no focus boost) and ≥ 30 attempts. It focuses on the two heaviest such keys,
    weakest first (`weakKeys`), plus the other keys `dueReviewKeys` says are due.
  - `remedialAllowed(recent, sessionIndex)` is the rate limit. A remedial is _offered_ at most
    once every 3 sessions, played or not. Offer slots are 3 sessions apart, counted from the last
    remedial played (it is offered again after two other sessions). With none played, the slots
    are sessions 0, 3, 6, …. A camp the child passes by therefore comes back three sessions later,
    never the next one, and the profile needs no "shown" record. Playing a camp again straight
    away (Results → Play again) is the child's choice and always allowed. So the limit is on
    offers, not on remedial sessions.
  - `remedialId(keys, sessionIndex)` makes `remedial-<cyrb53 of keys+session, base 36>`.
  - `remedialLesson(suggestion, anchor, layout)` builds the lesson: 60 s, Mine, `bandScale` 1 for
    every band, no `wpmRelative`. It takes the anchor's biome, order, kind, pools, policy and
    `caseInsensitiveFor`.
  - `remedialTitle` picks the title by the finger of the weakest key (`fingerFor` on the
    profile's layout).
  - No clock, no randomness. The inputs are the stats, the session index and the history's lesson
    ids.
- `src/app/recommend.ts` (new): `recommendNext(profile, lessons)`, `nextCurriculumLesson`,
  `dueRemedial`, `anchorLesson` (the furthest lesson reached, played or tested out),
  `currentBiome`, `campIsland` (the camp sits on the next curriculum lesson's island), `campLabel`,
  and `remedialById` (a small in-memory list of the last 8 remedials
  handed out, so the shell can resolve one for LessonIntro, play, Play again and Results).
  `openLessons`, `isOptional` and the skipped-challenge rule moved here from `map-logic.ts`, so the
  rules are in one place.
- `src/i18n/en/remedial.ts` (new namespace) plus one append-only line pair in `src/i18n/en/index.ts`:
  - per-finger titles ("Your left pinky wants practice!", "Your thumbs want practice!", and an
    "any" fallback);
  - the camp's screen-reader label, with a plural for "Practice key"/"Practice keys";
  - the tent's visible word "Practice" and LessonIntro's "Practice keys" heading;
  - "{a} and {b}" and the key names.
- **Approved hooks, filled in:** `src/ui/screens/map-logic.ts`
  - `recommendedLesson` returns `nextCurriculumLesson`, the curriculum half of `recommendNext`.
    So the pulsing stone, its "next" screen-reader label, the island's "Go here next!" and the
    first focus always follow the next lesson, whether or not a remedial is due (review B1).
  - `practiceCamp(profile, lessons, biome)` returns `{ label, lesson }` for the due remedial on the

## Decisions

- **Rate limit counts offers, played or not** (orchestrator, review B1). The slots are derived
  from the history (the sessions since the last remedial id) and the session counter, so no new
  save field is needed. An offer counts even when the child never opened the map that session
  (for example, they went straight from Results to the next lesson). It is still the same
  "at most once every 3 sessions".
- **Only clearly weak keys are focused.** The "top-2" are the two heaviest keys that both pass
  weight ≥ 4 and ≥ 30 attempts. With one such key, the remedial focuses on that one alone rather
  than adding a comfortable key.
- **Anchor = the furthest lesson reached** (played or tested out). The remedial copies its place,
  so Mine's `allowedKeys`, pools, blocks and wrong-key rules work unchanged, and Results' "next
  lesson" is the one after the anchor, which is always open.
- **Fewest stars:** the current biome (the anchor's) first, then any open lesson, as before.
  Tested-out lessons (`isTestedOut`) are skipped, because their one star is not a score.
- **The camp is an optional side stop** (orchestrator, review B1). Every "next" cue follows the
  next curriculum lesson. `recommendNext` still returns the due remedial first, as the card asks,
  but the map draws that remedial as the camp beside the next stone.
- **Title by finger of the weakest key only.** One finger keeps the sentence simple for Sprout.
  Space reads "Your thumbs want practice!".

## Notes for downstream tasks

- `remedialById` only knows remedials handed out since the page loaded. After a reload, a
  `remedial-…` deep link shows LessonIntro's friendly "missing" screen. The map makes a fresh one.
- A remedial's `order` equals its anchor's. Code that groups lessons by `biome` + `order` must use
  `app.lessons` (shipped only), never a list with a remedial in it.
