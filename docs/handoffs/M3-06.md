# M3-06 Remedial lessons, baselines and band defaults

Status: done

## What changed

- `src/engine/remedial.ts` (new, pure; covered by the `src/engine/**` pure-zone override): the
  remedial rules.
  - `suggestRemedial(input)` returns a suggestion when an allowed key has weight ≥ 4 (contracts §4
    `keyWeights`, with no focus boost) and ≥ 30 attempts. It focuses on the two heaviest such keys,
    weakest first (`weakKeys`), plus the other keys `dueReviewKeys` says are due.
  - `remedialAllowed(recent)` is the rate limit: no remedial in either of the last two sessions,
    so any 3 sessions in a row hold at most one.
  - `remedialId(keys, sessionIndex)` makes `remedial-<cyrb53 of keys+session, base 36>`.
  - `remedialLesson(suggestion, anchor, layout)` builds the lesson: 60 s, Mine, `bandScale` 1 for
    every band, no `wpmRelative`. It takes the anchor's biome, order, kind, pools, policy and
    `caseInsensitiveFor`.
  - `remedialTitle` picks the title by the finger of the weakest key (`fingerFor` on the
    profile's layout).
  - No clock, no randomness. The inputs are the stats, the session index and the history's lesson
    ids.
- `src/app/recommend.ts` (new): `recommendNext(profile, lessons)`, `nextCurriculumLesson`,
  `dueRemedial`, `anchorLesson` (the furthest lesson reached, played or tested out),
  `currentBiome`, `campLabel`, and `remedialById` (a small in-memory list of the last 8 remedials
  handed out, so the shell can resolve one for LessonIntro, play, Play again and Results).
  `openLessons`, `isOptional` and the skipped-challenge rule moved here from `map-logic.ts`, so the
  rules are in one place.
- `src/i18n/en/remedial.ts` (new namespace) plus one append-only line pair in `src/i18n/en/index.ts`:
  - per-finger titles ("Your left pinky wants practice!", "Your thumbs want practice!", and an
    "any" fallback);
  - the camp's screen-reader label, with a plural for "Practice key"/"Practice keys";
  - "{a} and {b}" and the key names.

  - `practiceCamp(profile, lessons, biome)` returns `{ label, lesson }` for the due remedial on its

## Decisions

- **Rate limit = played remedials.** The history counts sessions whose lesson id is a remedial
  id. A camp the child ignores stays on the path (it never nags beyond the tent and the island's
  "Go here next!" tag), and it changes id each session because the id includes the session.
- **Only clearly weak keys are focused.** The "top-2" are the two heaviest keys that both pass
  weight ≥ 4 and ≥ 30 attempts. With one such key, the remedial focuses on that one alone rather
  than adding a comfortable key.
- **Anchor = the furthest lesson reached** (played or tested out). The remedial copies its place,
  so Mine's `allowedKeys`, pools, blocks and wrong-key rules work unchanged, and Results' "next
  lesson" is the one after the anchor, which is always open.
- **Fewest stars:** the current biome (the anchor's) first, then any open lesson, as before.
  Tested-out lessons (`isTestedOut`) are skipped, because their one star is not a score.
- **No stone pulses while a remedial is due.** The camp is the recommendation. The island keeps
  "Go here next!". Keyboard focus still starts on the next new stone, and the camp is the stop
  before it.
- **Title by finger of the weakest key only.** One finger keeps the sentence simple for Sprout.
  Space reads "Your thumbs want practice!".
