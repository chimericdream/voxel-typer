What the numbers say:

- **No learner gets 0 stars in any lesson.** Under `stay`, every lesson that is finished earns ≥ 1 star. So lesson 1 always ends with a win.
- For the slow Sprout, 62–80% earn ≥ 2 stars in their first session, and most improve by session 5. Lesson 1 has the most 3-star results for slow typists (40% → 54%), so the first experience is the easiest.
- Fast typists earn 3 stars almost always. That is expected while the goal is accuracy only (PLAN.md §4.1).
- **Caveat: the simulator plays words and phrases as syllables.** `tools/sim.ts` calls the generator with `pools: []` (M1-07 had no word lists yet), so rows 05 and 06 measure home-row syllables at those lengths, not the real words. The words are mostly 3–5 letters of the same keys, so the difficulty should be close, but it isn't measured. Fixing this means passing the pools in `tools/sim.ts`, which this card doesn't own (see Notes).

## Decisions

- **Lesson 04 is `letters`, not `syllables`** (the card allows "letters/syllables"). With `a` as the only vowel, syllables fill up with `a`, and the comfort cap (contracts §4) then leaves `;` about 5% of the chars, even though `;` is a focus key. A Sprout session (36 chars) had no `;` at all in 37 of 200 seeds. As letters, `;` gets ~15% and `a` ~17%, against ~11% for each older key. A Sprout session misses a new key in 2 of 1000 seeds.
- **Lesson 03 is `syllables`** ("letters → syllables"). One lesson has one `kind`, so it can't switch mid-lesson. With no vowel yet, the syllables are 2–3 key groups such as `sfk` and `lds`. `s` and `l` get ~19–20% of chars each, against ~15% for the older keys.
- **Lesson 06 is `phrases` from a new list, not `words`.** The card says "home-row words longer, short review". Lessons 1–6 only have `a s d f j k l ;` (`g h` come later, README §8.4), and only 21 real words use those keys (`home-row.txt` minus the `g`/`h` words). Only ~10 of them are 4–5 letters. A "longer words" pool couldn't reach 20 distinct targets, and `Lesson` has no `minLen`. Two or three home-row words in a row (`dad adds salsa`) make longer targets from the same words, give ≥ 20 distinct targets, and review every key. The title is "Word Pairs". If the card meant a plain `words` lesson, it is a two-line change: `kind: "words"`, `pools: ["home-row"]`.
- **Lesson 05 uses `home-row`**, which is already tagged `biome=meadow`. Lessons 05 and 06 name their pools explicitly, so each gets only its own list. Otherwise the default ("pools tagged with the biome") would mix phrases into the words lesson.
- **`home-row.txt` is not extended.** I looked for more real words in `a s d f j k l` that a 6-year-old knows and found none that pass review. The rest are excluded in `SOURCES.md` (`lass`, `sass`, `alas`, `flak`, …) or too long for `band=all` (`salads`). `la` and `fa` (music notes) were candidates, but they add little. Lesson 05 has 21 typeable words for every band, and the test requires ≥ 20.
- **Lesson 01 is shorter than the rest:** `bandScale` 0.5 for Sprout (25 chars, about 25 key presses of `f` and `j`) and 1.2 for Builder (60). It is tagged `intro`, the only lesson tagged so. Only `f` and `j` are allowed, and the generator alternates them in runs of at most 3.
- **`reviewKeys` names the pair just before** (lesson 02 reviews `f j`, 03 reviews `d k`, 04 reviews `s l`), and all eight keys for 05 and 06. The contract says the allowed set is wider anyway (all keys so far). Today `reviewKeys` affects nothing: `keyWeights` raises only `focus` (= `newKeys`).
- **i18n namespace is `lessonsMeadow`** (file `lessons-meadow.ts`), keys `meadow01` … `meadow06`. The `;` title says "A and ;", because the keycap shows `;`. A child doesn't need to read the word "semicolon".
- **Phrase list choices:** no `sad` or `falls` (lesson 6 should feel happy, and falling is a hurt), no `fad` (most 6-year-olds don't know it), no pairs that make no sense. Recorded in `SOURCES.md`.

## Notes for downstream tasks

- **M2-11 (lesson intro):** the card's example goal "Find the F and J bumps!" has no field on `Lesson` and no key here. `tools/i18n-keys.ts` fails on unused keys, so I added only titles. If the intro screen wants a per-lesson goal line, either M2-11 adds `goal` strings in `screens.ts`, keyed by lesson id through a ``t(`screens.goal.${id}`)`` prefix, or a `goalKey` goes on `Lesson` (a contract change).
- **M2-13 / M2-15:** lesson ids are `meadow-01` … `meadow-06`. Lesson 06 targets contain spaces inside the target (`dad adds salsa`), so `typeLikeAKid` must type the inner spaces as part of the target.
- **M3-01 (`allowedKeysFor`, curriculum validator):** the test computes the allowed set itself (cumulative Meadow `newKeys`), because `allowedKeysFor` is still a stub. When it lands, the test's `allowedFor` can call it. The result must be identical for these lessons.
- **Words lessons never practice `j` or `;`.** No real English word in these keys has them, so after lesson 04, `j` and `;` get no practice until a lesson brings letters or syllables back. Meadow's later lessons (or an M3 review lesson) should drill them.
- **The comfort cap limits new keys.** New keys are top-weight keys, so the cap keeps them at ≤ 50% of the chars together. In lesson 02 that gives `d` and `k` ~24% each, against ~27% each for `f` and `j`. A learner meeting two new keys types them a bit *less* than the old ones. That is the generator and adaptive design (M1-05, M1-06), not lesson data. Worth a look in M3 tuning.
- **`tools/sim.ts` ignores word pools** (`pools: []`). Passing the lesson's pools (`poolsFromTexts` on the `.txt` files) would let the simulator measure words and phrases lessons for real.

## Contract change request / Questions
